Privacy Policy — ishowspeedvibecut

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Information We Access

ishowspeedvibecut (VibeCut) uses official OAuth 2.0 authorization from Google (YouTube Data API v3, scope youtube.upload) and TikTok (Login Kit & Content Posting API, scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish) solely when explicitly authorized by the channel owner.

2. How Data Is Used

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the creator's own server (/data/accounts.json) and used exclusively to upload vertical video clips initiated by the creator.
  • ishowspeedvibecut does not sell, rent, or share user data or OAuth credentials with third parties.
  • ishowspeedvibecut's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Retention & Revocation

Creators may revoke access at any time via their Google Account Permissions or TikTok App Authorizations, or delete stored tokens directly from their local instance.

4. Contact

For questions or data deletion requests, contact ishowspeedvibecut@gmail.com.
