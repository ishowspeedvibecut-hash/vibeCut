ishowspeedvibecut

ishowspeedvibecut (VibeCut) is a creator workflow tool that transforms long gaming livestreams into vertical 9:16 highlight clips with synchronized captions and lets authorized channel owners publish clips directly to their own YouTube Shorts and TikTok accounts.

Key Features

  • Automated detection of peak gaming moments from creator livestreams.
  • 9:16 vertical formatting with burned-in captions and live chat context.
  • Creator-initiated publishing to connected YouTube and TikTok channels via official OAuth 2.0 APIs.
